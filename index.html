The chart shows one coin's price over the last 7 days. The line going up means the price rose; going down means it fell. It's just a picture of history — it doesn't predict the future.

The table lists coins ranked by market cap (see the "?" next to that column) — roughly, the biggest/most-established coins are at the top.

Market Cap The total value of every coin in circulation (price × how many exist). A rough way to compare how big/established one coin is versus another — bigger usually (not always) means more stable.
